ABOUT SELEMO
LEADS DIRECT

FOOTPRINTS >

In July 2023, the Managing Director realized there was a gap in the Southern African sales market. While reflecting, it became increasingly evident that the sales strategies in the market have historically been quite traditional, lacking a scientific approach and somewhat resembling the direct, personal methods used by most door-to-door sales and marketing strategists. This realization underscored the potential for transformative change in the region's sales landscape through the adoption of advanced technological solutions such as AI and others. This, coupled with the persistent demand for exceptional customer experiences birthed the idea of Selemo Leads Direct. Now that the animal, as we affectionately call it, had been birthed!
